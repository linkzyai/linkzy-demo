title: "Planning content around your website pages"
---

Articles work hardest when they support the main pages of a website. An article that explains a problem should point the reader to the service page that solves it, so the visit does not end when the article does.

A simple way to plan is to list the website's most important pages and write down three questions a visitor might have before they are ready to use each one. Every question is a possible article with a clear place to send readers afterwards.

Looking at how visitors move from articles to service pages shows which pieces are pulling their weight. An article with plenty of readers and no onward clicks often just needs a clearer link to the next page.
